The phenomenological approach adopted in this qualitative investigation emphasizes the subjective lived experiences of healthcare practitioners within emergency departments. Semi-structured interviews were transcribed verbatim and analyzed thematically using Braun and Clarke's reflexive framework. Analysis revealed three overarching themes: systemic workload compression, emotional fatigue, and informal coping strategies mediated through peer debriefing. The findings suggest that institutional support structures often lag behind the emotional demands placed upon clinical staff during peak hospital admissions.
